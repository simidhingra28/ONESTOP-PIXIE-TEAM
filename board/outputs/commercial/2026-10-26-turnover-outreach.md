**Flags for Mushroom**

## 3. Phone script (about 30 seconds)

> Hi, is this [Name / the office]? It's Mushroom from OneStop KWC, a local home services company here in Kitchener.
>
> Quick one. Oct 31 is a big move-out day, and we still have openings for move-out cleans Oct 31 to Nov 2, anywhere in Kitchener, Waterloo or Cambridge.
>
> Do you have any units turning over this weekend?

**If yes:** "Great. What's the address and unit size? I'll quote it now and book a time while we're on the phone."

**If no / not the right person:** "No problem. Who books cleaners for your turnovers? ... Can I send them our number? It's 519-722-9057, answered 24/7."

**If "how much?":** "Depends on size and condition. Tell me the unit and I'll give you a price right now." (Mushroom quotes live. Agents don't.)

**Realtor opener:** swap line 2 for "If you've got a listing going up, we can clean it before photos and showings this week."
