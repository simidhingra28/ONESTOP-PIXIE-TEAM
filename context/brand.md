# OneStop KWC — brand rules

## Look
- Logo: stop-sign octagon with house / "1" mark
- Colours: red and black. White space. No pastel, no stock-photo blue.
- Posters: 1080×1350 px (Instagram portrait)

## Voice
- Warm, plainspoken, local. Sounds like a neighbour who knows a good tradesperson.
- Short sentences. No em dashes. No filler ("we're thrilled", "look no further", "your one-stop solution").
- Name real places: Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Uptown Waterloo, Downtown Kitchener, Galt, Hespeler, Preston.
- Rotate the area name: KWC / Kitchener-Waterloo / Kitchener-Waterloo-Cambridge.
- Line we own: "Before you call your dad."

## Always
- One clear call to action: call 519-722-9057 or book at onestopkwc.ca
- Mention 24/7 phone when it helps (urgent plumbing, electrical)

## Never
- Invented reviews, names, stats, photos, or "trusted by thousands"
- Prices not in context/company.md
- Stacked discounts or small % codes
