# Boost note: is a small geo-targeted boost worth it?
Status: PROPOSAL. Nothing spent. Mushroom decides.

## Recommendation
Yes, one small boost, on Facebook only. Not Instagram.
- Boost: the Tuesday Facebook page post (Kitchener version, or the poster with the phone CTA) from the OneStop KWC page.
- Budget: $45 CAD total. $15 per day, Tue 10-27 to Thu 10-29. Ads Manager will show the final figure.
- Placement: Facebook feed only. Turn off Instagram, Audience Network and Messenger placements. This keeps out the non-Canadian IG audience.
- Geo: Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Ontario only. Use "People living in this location", not "recently in". Add a radius around each city centre of about 10 km, or add the three cities by name. Exclude everything else.
- Audience: age 22 to 65, no interest stacking.
- Objective: Calls (or Messages if calls are unavailable). Not Engagement.

## Expected reach
I have no OneStop ad history, so I cannot quote a real number. Planning assumption only: at roughly $10 to $20 per 1,000 people reached in a small Ontario market, $45 reaches about 2,000 to 4,500 people. Treat this as an unverified estimate. Ads Manager shows an estimated daily reach before you confirm, so use that figure instead of mine.

## Expected result
Very uncertain. A tiny audience of people who are moving this week means 0 to 1 booking is realistic, plus a few calls. The goal of 5 bookings by Fri cannot rest on this boost. It is a cheap test of whether paid local reach beats unpaid group posts, and it also seeds local followers.

## Risks
- Meta ad review can take hours. Submit Tuesday morning.
- Housing-adjacent ads can face extra review, but a cleaning service should be fine.
- If group posts already bring calls by Tue evening, skip the boost.
